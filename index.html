North Addon Bot

A private Discord bot of the North Addon Team

What it does

North Addon Bot posts game addons (mods, presets, sound packs) into the North Addon Discord server. Files that are too large to attach to a Discord message are uploaded to a Google Drive account owned by the North Addon Team, and a download link is placed in the Discord post.

How it uses Google Drive

The bot asks for one permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, the bot can create a folder called "North Addon" and upload files into that folder, and turn on "anyone with the link" sharing for those files. It cannot see, open, change or delete any other file in the Drive. The bot is used by a single account, the one owned by the North Addon Team. It has no public sign-up and does not ask other people to log in.
